// Strict Studio · Business Development

GAME ACQUISITIONS

We acquire quality Roblox games and invest in their future. Whether you want a clean exit or want to stay on as lead developer — we have a deal structure built for you.

// Choose Your Path

ACQUISITION PLANS

🏆

FULL ACQUISITION

Option 1 · Complete Ownership Transfer

You sell your game and Strict Studio takes full ownership. Development, staff, monetization — everything transfers to us. You walk away with a clean payout and zero obligations.

  • One-time lump sum negotiated upfront
  • Studio handles all future development & updates
  • Studio manages all staff and operating costs
  • Clean exit — no ongoing obligations
  • No role in the game after handoff
  • No share of future revenue
APPLY ON DISCORD
🤝

PARTNERSHIP MODEL

Option 2 · Co-Ownership + 50% Revenue Share

Strict Studio acquires and fully funds the game. You stay on as Lead Developer, build your own team under our banner, and keep 50% of all revenue to share with your team however you decide.

  • Studio funds all development costs
  • You remain Lead Developer with creative control
  • Hire & manage your own team under our brand
  • 50% revenue share — distribute freely to your team
  • Studio handles publishing, marketing & operations
  • Strict Studio holds ownership & final decisions

// What Happens Next

THE PROCESS

1

Reach Out on Discord

Open a ticket or DM us. Share your game name, genre, and rough stats.

2

Game Review

Our team evaluates your game — visits, CCU, retention, and monetization. Response within 48–72 hours.

3

Offer & Plan Selection

If we're interested, we send a formal offer. You choose Full or Partnership and we negotiate terms.

4

Agreement & Transfer

We draft a written agreement. Once signed, ownership transfers and funding begins immediately.

5

Launch Under Strict Studio

The game continues under our brand. Marketing, updates, and growth begin right away.
