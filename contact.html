Common questions.

Do you take commissioned game work?

Sometimes. We only take projects that fit the team and give us a clear role in development.

Can creators make videos about your games?

Yes. Contact the community team for press material or access to upcoming playtests.

Are you hiring?

Open roles are posted through our community channels. Portfolios can also be sent to the careers email.

Can I send you a game idea?

We do not accept standalone game ideas. Business pitches should include a team, plan, or clear partnership.
